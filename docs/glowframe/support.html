What does the score mean?

The 0–100 Beauty score is a facial-attractiveness game result based on a formula chosen by the developer. An app-defined beauty score. Not a scientific assessment. It is not a population percentile or a measure of your worth, personality, or health. Different photos and detected landmarks can produce different results.

How does the formula work?

Apple Vision detects your face and facial landmarks on your iPhone. The app's geometry calculations then produce Feature alignment, Eye balance, and Outline balance components and combine them into the overall score. AI performs detection only. The scoring formula is developer-defined and has not been trained or validated to measure human attractiveness.

How do I get started?

  1. Read the score explanation and photo-use consent.
  2. Select your own image with the photo picker, or take your own selfie.
  3. Use a clearly visible single face and explore the Beauty score, Feature alignment, Eye balance, and Outline balance.
  4. Reset when you want to clear the current result or start again.

Why is my photo rejected?

GlowFrame requires one detectable face. Try a clear, evenly lit photo with only your own face visible. Face the camera directly and keep both eyes visible. Photos with no detected face, more than one face, a turned or tilted-away face, or insufficient visible landmarks may be rejected. If an image cannot be loaded, try selecting it again or choose another image.

Can I use it without camera permission?

You can use the system photo picker without taking a new selfie. If you want to enable camera access later, open your iPhone's Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Camera, and find GlowFrame.

Are my photos uploaded?

Face and landmark detection and the app-defined scoring formula run on your iPhone. The app has no photo-upload service, account, analytics, or advertising. Your current photo and result stay in app memory, without a saved history, and are cleared by reset or app process termination. Switching to another app does not necessarily terminate the process.

What happens when I share?

The generated PNG card omits your face photo by default. You can choose to include it for that result. The iOS share sheet then lets you select a destination or cancel. Nothing is posted automatically. Once you save or send a card, resetting GlowFrame does not delete the resulting copy.

Does GlowFrame cost anything?

GlowFrame is free, with no subscriptions, in-app purchases, or advertising.
