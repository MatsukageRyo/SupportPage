GlowFrame Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026
Operator: Japan Industrial Tech Successions Inc. (株式会社日本産業技術承継)

GlowFrame uses facial landmarks from your own selfie to create an app-defined beauty score, just for fun, on your iPhone. We do not receive your photo, face measurements, or score. Sharing is optional and sends a result card only to a destination you choose.

What the app processes

When you select a photo or take a selfie, Apple Vision detects your face and facial landmarks on your device. A developer-defined geometry formula uses those points to calculate Feature alignment, Eye balance, Outline balance, and a 0–100 Beauty score. AI is used for detection only; the score is not produced by a trained or validated attractiveness model. An app-defined beauty score. Not a scientific assessment. The app does not identify people or infer personality, demographic attributes, or health.

On-device processing

The app has no account, photo-upload service, backend, advertising, analytics, subscription, or in-app purchase. Photos, face measurements, and results are not sent to the operator or an external AI service. The system photo picker provides the image you select; it does not give GlowFrame unrestricted access to your photo library. Camera use requires your permission.

Retention and clearing

The current image and result are kept in app memory, without a saved photo or result history. Resetting clears that current analysis, and ending the app process clears its memory. Merely switching to another app may leave GlowFrame suspended in memory. Resetting does not delete the original image from your photo library or any copies you have saved or shared.

The app generates the PNG share card in memory and passes it to the system share sheet without writing a temporary image file. iOS or the destination you select may retain a separate copy as part of sharing or saving.

Optional sharing

GlowFrame creates a PNG card when you choose to share. Your photo is omitted by default. You can opt to include it for that result, then choose a destination in the iOS share sheet. Nothing is posted automatically. Review the card before sending it.

If you choose to save a result card to Photos, iOS may request permission to add that card to your photo library. This permission is used for the save action you choose; the app does not automatically save photos or results.

The chosen app, recipient, or service receives the shared card and may store or redistribute it under its own practices. Clearing GlowFrame cannot recall or delete those copies. A card without the photo can still contain the score and result text.

Your choices

You can cancel photo selection, decline camera access, reset the current result, and choose not to share. Manage camera access in your iPhone settings. Use your own photo and include it in a shared card only when you intend the recipient to receive it.

Support messages and this website

If you email support, Japan Industrial Tech Successions Inc. receives the email address and information you voluntarily provide and uses them to respond to your inquiry. Avoid sending a face photo with your initial request. Support correspondence is separate from the app's on-device analysis. You can contact us about your support correspondence or a deletion request.

These support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting the website is separate from using the app. The hosting service may process request information under its own privacy practices. These pages contain no analytics scripts or contact forms.
