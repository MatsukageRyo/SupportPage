Save a daily bookmark

Choose a mood, write a note of up to 2,000 characters, or do both. You can save one entry per day. Open an entry in the reflection tab to edit or delete it.

Prepare for a plan

Add a trip, meeting, or personal plan from the first tab. Tap checklist items to mark them complete or incomplete. Use the plan's menu to edit its details or delete it.

Change the language

Star Bookmark includes English and Japanese. It follows your app language preference in iPhone Settings. The location and wording of this setting vary by iOS version.

If notifications do not appear

Check the app's notification permission in iPhone Settings and the notification switch inside the app. Focus and other device settings can affect delivery. Notifications mark the approximate start and end of Mercury retrograde; they are not reminders for individual plans. You can use the journal without allowing notifications.

Your records and backups

Records are saved locally in the app. You can export them as a JSON file from Settings and choose where to keep it. Version 1.0.0 does not import exported files. Depending on your device settings, app data may be included in iCloud or computer backups. The developer does not operate a storage or recovery service for your records.

Delete your data

Delete individual entries or plans, or use the delete-all option in Settings. The app cannot undo deletion. Exported files, shared copies, and existing backups must be deleted separately.

If records cannot be loaded or saved

Check your device's available storage and any error shown. If existing records cannot be loaded, the app stops saving to protect the original file. Export it for safekeeping before choosing whether to delete all data.

Mercury retrograde and reflection

The bundled astronomical data covers 2020 through 2040. Times use your device's time zone and are approximate; calculation methods can differ by a few minutes. Astrology is presented as a traditional symbolic interpretation, not a guarantee of future events. Mood summaries describe your saved entries and do not show that planetary motion causes your feelings.

Compatibility: iPhone with iOS 17 or later. All features are free, with no ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.
