Privacy Policy

Star Bookmark (星のしおり) is an iPhone app provided by Japan Industrial Tech Successions Inc. (株式会社日本産業技術承継) for preparing for plans and recording your daily moods and experiences.

Information on your device

Your plans, checklist items, journal entries, moods, entry dates and times, and notification settings are processed and stored on your device to provide the app's features. No account is required. The app does not send these records to a developer-operated server. It does not read your contacts, location, or system calendars.

Advertising, analytics, and tracking

The app includes no advertising, analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking. The developer does not collect your activity within the app.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, the app schedules local reminders for the approximate start and end of Mercury retrograde. No remote push notification server is used. You can change notification permission in iPhone Settings at any time. The journal works without notification permission.

Exporting and sharing

Exporting creates a JSON file containing your records. You choose where to save or share it. The file may contain personal information, so check its contents and destination. The policies of any app or service you choose apply to its handling of the exported file. Version 1.0.0 does not import exported files.

Backups

Depending on your device settings and use, app data may be included in iCloud or computer backups. The app has no developer-operated synchronization service. Manage or delete backups through Apple or your chosen backup destination.

Retention and deletion

Your records remain in the app until you delete them. You can delete individual entries and plans or use the delete-all option in Settings. Removing the app normally removes its local app data. Exported files, shared copies, and existing backups must be managed separately. The developer cannot view or restore the records on your device.
